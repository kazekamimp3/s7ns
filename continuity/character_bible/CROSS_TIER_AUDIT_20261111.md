## 3. Cross-tier identity and mechanics checks

### Confirmed consistent

1. **Kaze / Lobo:** Kaze’s heart is Kaze’s; it is in Lobo’s saddlebag. The scanner effect belongs to Lobo carrying that heart. It does not convert the heart into Lobo’s organ. The 1,502 total preserves the 1,501 fee + one-Brapple tip.
2. **Bee Moo / Mini-Adult Brian:** two distinct characters in separate continuity contexts. Tier 2 explicitly prohibits importing the conjoined miniature Brian into Bee Moo’s Act 35–45 identity. Tier 5 keeps the conjoined pair separate.
3. **Chris / Catfish Billy:** two autonomous minds, one exterior body, no simultaneous exterior duplicate. This is not the Brusta / Mini-Brian body.
4. **Daynah / Mahnah:** Daynah is Mahnah’s primary owner/handler; Mahnah remains an Earth blue-nose pit bull, nonverbal and without Ki. Mana’s spatial-bite mechanics do not transfer to Billy, Chris or Daynah.
5. **Batuna / Marcelina vs Denise:** Batuna/Marcelina is the dead four-ton Nal Hutta corpse; Denise is the living human woman encountered beneath the Lānaʻi bridge. They are not one character or one body.
6. **Bob / Micah:** two independent consciousnesses in one shared host body in the S3 history. Bob’s locked final words are not assigned to Micah. No second external body is created.
7. **K.K.:** current Act 25 blood-thorn casualty/regrowth pending is kept separate from the older S3 seed-birth and Maul E46 events.
8. **Shake / Frylock:** the current Space Saga Act 1 Shake death and Frylock unknown whereabouts are kept separate from older S3 future / living-BELLY states.
9. **Brusta / Mini-Adult Brian:** two independent minds in one conjoined body; not merged into one speaker. Brusta’s mistaken Busta Rhymes label is not used to import the real rapper’s biography.
10. **Future acts:** Jabba’s Act 44 meeting/lawsuit and Bee Moo’s Act 45 confrontation with Kaze remain queued development, not completed events.

After these identity, status, alias, body-sharing and event-order checks, no additional direct contradiction between the five tier documents and the November 11 Act 42 checkpoint was demonstrated.
