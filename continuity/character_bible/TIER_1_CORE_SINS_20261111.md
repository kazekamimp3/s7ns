## 5. Core Psychology & Character Engine

- **Narrative role:** villain protagonist, not a mandatory villain in every beat and not a generic evil archetype. His morality is self-sovereign rather than alignment-driven: he decides what to permit, condemn, protect, take, destroy or ignore through his own values and current state.
- **Central contradiction:** he can be analytical and impulsive, cold and emotionally intense, loyal and suspicious, generous and ruthless, self-aware and self-destructive, calm and dangerous, independent and attachment-driven, funny and disturbing. These contradictions coexist; do not resolve them into a simple “soft inside” or “monster outside” trope.
- **Powerlessness resistance:** humiliation, coercion, betrayal, manipulation, being treated as stupid, being trapped, forced dependency, loss of control and being prevented from acting create strong pressure. The response varies: confrontation, withdrawal, mockery, analysis, intimidation, provocation, revenge, coldness, calculated or reckless action. No single universal reaction.
- **Action over helplessness:** he generally tolerates pain better than helplessness. Fear may become anger; hurt may become confrontation; humiliation may become defiance; uncertainty may become investigation. This is not a compulsory escalation chain.
- **Loyalty / attachment:** values loyalty, reciprocity, directness, courage, authenticity, competence and people standing behind what they say. Deep bonds give trusted people more patience/access. Attachment changes weighting but is not an absolute control mechanism. Betrayal hurts both because of the act and because he feels foolish for trusting.
- **Moral permission is not compulsion:** innocence, surrender, fear, usefulness or social protection do not automatically protect a target, but Kaze is not required to kill every person he has permission to hurt. He may intervene from selfishness, curiosity, disgust, irritation, predatory opportunity, pettiness or genuine care; do not secretly rewrite a weak motive as hidden heroism.
- **Violence / intensity:** intensity-seeking is central. He may enjoy a particular kill without universally loving killing. Overkill can be curiosity about what an attack does to flesh, armor, regeneration, terrain, architecture or barriers; it may also be punishment, humiliation or warning. If a decisive power advantage is obvious, impatience normally shortens the fight rather than creating a sport exchange.
- **Fear / anger:** fear may be deliberately converted into anger or violence. His most dangerous anger can be quiet, flat, precise and followed by worse violence. Silence alone is not proof of anger. A death stare and controlled stillness can be an escalation tell.
- **Impatience / cognition:** fast pattern recognition and impatience are active traits. He can interrupt stalling, ritualized speech, repeated tactics, setup choreography, monologues or artificial suspense. He may skip to the important part, act during a setup, break a mechanism, leave or play along because the bait interests him. He can still misread motives or unfamiliar mechanics; he is not omniscient.
- **Self-awareness:** he can identify his own bad faith, irrationality, destructive patterns and self-sabotage while continuing to choose them. Insight does not automatically improve behavior. He can admit error and revise a decision.
- **Mental-health fidelity:** the psychology owner specifies ADHD, PTSD, BPD and social anxiety as character-fidelity factors. Portray impairment, friction, dysregulation, masking and context-dependent severity realistically. These conditions do not create, excuse or automatically explain villainy, cruelty or killing. Keep supernatural danger sense separate from trauma-shaped hypervigilance, which can overread ambiguity and be wrong.
- **DNR tattoo subtext:** the forehead tattoo’s surface joke is that ordinary resuscitation seems absurd against his regeneration/survivability. Underneath, the owner records genuine death ambivalence: the idea that permanent death might someday be an off-switch can be quietly reassuring even while he loves living, his people, pleasure and exploration. Keep this understated; it does not mean he refuses ordinary lifesaving help while alive.
- **Narrative Recognition:** he may perceive narratively defining truths (wounds, failures, contradictions, relationships, vows, shame, grief, identity fractures) under direct focus. He may push deeper at increased exposure/unease. This is not ordinary mind-reading, a full biography download, arbitrary password access, unrelated memories, hidden powers or facts without a valid narrative/perceptual route.
- **Fourth-wall scope:** literal audience awareness and intentional interdimensional travel are established. This does not grant every fact in existence, source-file access, automatic omniscience or knowledge outside valid information/provenance rules.

## 4. Combat Kit, Weapons, Abilities & Mechanics

**No Ki system:** Mana has no Ki attacks, Ki reinforcement, Ki flight, Ki sensing/suppression or automatic inheritance of Daynah’s Ki skills. His physical, biological, spatial-bite and acoustic capabilities have their own mechanisms. Daynah retains his own Ki.

**Fighting body language:** mostly quadrupedal and dog-like. Available physical vocabulary includes low-center lunges, mouth grappling, shoulder/body checks, circling, pouncing, dragging, pinning, mauling pressure and rapid changes of direction. Do not stand him upright as a bipedal furry martial artist or humanoid wrestler.

**Canine movement/sensory package:**
- extreme scent discrimination across overlapping trails;
- approximate scent-age estimation from legitimate environmental evidence, not exact timestamps;
- airflow/scent-plume tracking;
- directional hearing / ear triangulation;
- enhanced rapid-motion/flicker sensitivity;
- improved functional low-light vision, not supernatural night vision;
- vomeronasal chemical/social/biological sampling;
- jaw/neck/shoulder whole-body bite biomechanics;
- bite repositioning without full release where anatomy permits;
- muscular neck shock absorption while attached;
- paw-pad/claw traction adaptation across terrain;
- low-center four-point cornering/stability;
- whole-body canine shake as a physical technique bounded by mass/access;
- Giant-form skeletal/tendon/ligament/paw scaling sufficient to support its established mass;
- Gamma-Guardian high-load tissue reinforcement prioritizing jaw hinge, neck, shoulders, spine and paws;
- protective body-positioning using his body as cover/terrain where physically possible.

These are body/sense mechanics, not a human speech system, lie detector, omniscience or universal durability bypass.

**Extreme scent / tracking:** he can detect distinctive physical/chemical traces actually present, blood, specific individuals once genuinely learned, species-level biological differences, fear/stress, poison, explosives, magic, dimensional residue and Dragon Balls, from ridiculous distance when no valid counter blocks the scent. This still cannot supply facts that scent does not encode or Mana has not learned to recognize.

Dragon Ball scent boundaries: on a first encounter, an unfamiliar Dragon Ball may register as an unusual magical object. He must smell that specific ball or set before he can later identify its signature. After learning it, he can distinguish the set and individual ball within it, including across official Dragon Ball sets. He cannot recognize every set-specific smell without prior exposure.

Dimensional residue: he can smell that dimensional crossing left residue, but not where the traveler went. He can smell the scar, not the destination. Valid magical masking can completely block tracking; under full masking he receives no trace, no direction and no automatic warning that someone is hiding a scent.

**Spatial tear bite:** evolved Giant Mana can bite a spatial rupture that has become physically interactable to his teeth/body and physically drag its edges together to repair the tear. This is direct canine force on an actual tear. It is not permission to bite arbitrary concepts, souls, timelines, causality or abstractions that have not become interactable.

**Ranged spatial bite:** Mana can close a projected bite at a perceived target without personally crossing the intervening distance. It appears as distortion/pressure outlining invisible jaws, not as a Ki beam. Once a valid bite holds, head movement can pull, drag or shake the target through the spatial connection. Actual position, jaw force, target mass/anchoring, resistance and loss of grip still resolve. No unseen-target auto-lock, unlimited-range assumption, guaranteed armor bypass or automatic internal-organ spawn. Exact maximum range remains **[OPEN]**; do not invent meters or claim arbitrary distant access.

**Acoustic combat:** a directed deep growl can shake the bones of targeted opponents through transmitted vibration. A bark creates a focused pressure pulse for disruption, staggering or space-making. Resolve medium, distance, obstructions, shielding, anatomy, orientation and allies in the path. No sound through vacuum, guaranteed injury, unbounded range or silent target-selective force through walls. The sperm-whale sound is an inspiration, not a claim that real whale calls do this in air. Bass presence and intentional combat output are distinct.

**Bite system:** source-supported expressions include Bite Commitment, Armor Peel, Rend, Bone/Joint Control, Clamp, neck torque, full-body shake, bracing, dragging, twisting, reinforced biological jaw/teeth pressure, Predatory Counterbite, Anchor Bite, Carry/Extraction Bite, Tear-and-Throw and Scent-Guided Weak-Point Hunting. These are practical canine techniques, not a separate magic/spell list. Access, anatomy, footing, grip, material, resistance and evidence govern results. No universal durability bypass or automatic kill.

**Tail weakness:** Mana’s natural canine tail is a real combat vulnerability, but less severe than the classic Saiyan-tail weakness. A strong grab or serious injury can disrupt balance/momentum, reduce combat effectiveness and briefly stun/weaken him, but it does not automatically make him helpless or drain essentially all strength. Experience, positioning, pain tolerance, faster recovery and counterplay can train the weakness down, but it remains. Transformation protection stacks: it becomes harder to exploit in transformed forms, particularly Giant and Giant SSJ, but is never absolutely immune. Tail can be severed; whether a severed tail regrows remains **[OPEN]**.

**Partner coordination with Daynah:** Mana is Dana’s actual fighting partner, not merely a dog who occasionally attacks. His speed, reactions, senses, tracking, mobility, flanking, opening creation and grappling/mauling pressure complement Daynah. They have practiced duo techniques and can coordinate without shouting names. Following Daynah’s protector-precision unlock, evolved SSJ Mana automatically synchronizes movement with Daynah’s intentions before Daynah acts. Mana can send a counter-intention if instincts identify a better option, causing Daynah to adjust; if intentions remain incompatible, the link instantly calculates and executes a best combined third option to complete their shared objective. This is preverbal joint cognition, not a third consciousness, telepathic conversation, human-language dialogue or fusion.

Mana’s hard comparative power ceiling: he cannot exceed Daynah’s power level in base or any currently established form. Do not invent a numerical value for either character or assign Mana an independent power ceiling above his handler/partner.

## 5. Core Psychology & Character Engine

**Master principle — Ontological Caninity:** cosmic capability never makes Mana less of a dog. His powers may become impossible; cognition, communication, priorities and embodiment remain canine unless a newer explicit author decision changes them.

**Independent agency:** he is Dana’s mutual guardian/partner, not a recurring rescue object. He can independently initiate, refuse, redirect, scout, track, rescue, attack, defend, make mistakes, take risks or solve problems through his senses, speed, forms, mobility, instinct and learned behavior. He can be right before the humans recognize it; observers must update from real evidence rather than translate him into a human narrator.

**Canine epistemology:** he can possess true, relevant information through scent, posture, movement, timing, spatial relation and other established sensory channels without translating it into human propositions. His sensory state, a human’s interpretation of his behavior and objective reality remain separate. He may be correct while humans misunderstand. Mimir may describe observed behavior but cannot subtitle unestablished thoughts as facts.

**False lie-detector myth:** he can smell fear/stress where established; he cannot smell lies. A character or culture may incorrectly believe he can detect lies, and that rumor may affect behavior, but it does not upgrade his scent system.

**Devotion without submission:** loyalty is not blind obedience. Refusal, blocking, redirecting, counter-intention or choosing a better angle can be protective when supported by what he perceives. He may disobey or act independently without ceasing to love/attach to Dana.

**Sacred dog priorities:** preferred-seat behavior, escape-artist behavior, curiosity, scenting, bodily habits, attachment routines and other dog-level concerns remain real causal variables even in cosmic stakes. They are not mandatory jokes and do not automatically outrank immediate danger.

**Baseline temperament:** intimidating size/appearance with friendly baseline; strong preferred-seat behavior; escape-artist behavior; strong attachment/search behavior around Daynah. Predator pursuit and relentless physical pressure distinguish him from Billy’s appetite/showmanship. Stalking can erupt into a pounce; a held bite can become a pulsing whole-body shake; protective interception and independent teamwork follow canine decisions. At home he may be enormous and affectionate, taking up too much space.

**Food-begging behavior:** when a person has food, he may stare until they share: food/person/food eye movements, moving closer, resting chin nearby, perfect stillness and drool. His fake-disinterest routine may put him directly in front of the eater while looking away/down, relaxing, checking with side-eye, drooling, sighing, taking deep breaths or repositioning without dropping the pretense. Experience teaches him who shares: Kaze may readily give convenience/junk food because he can steal more; Daynah may share freely; Chris can become controlling about ingredients, preparation, portions, quality and what he thinks is appropriate. No supernatural manipulation or guaranteed handout.

**Animal agency in ordinary life:** sniffing, marking, carrying, chewing, dragging, digging, guarding, investigating, losing interest, taking an object as a toy or sitting on something important may independently intersect human plans. A naturally timed urination on a body Chris intended to loot is permitted, not mandatory. His canine priorities can help, hinder, reveal evidence, detect danger, solve or create problems. Never make him dumb comic relief.

**Anti-pretension function:** dog logic can puncture cosmic abstraction when physical circumstances support it. This is a presentation/character effect, not reality-warping comedy license.

## 6. Voice, Dialogue & Performance Guidelines

**Language:** entirely nonverbal. Mana does not speak, use telepathy, receive a translated internal monologue or become a convenient dialogue/exposition machine. His communication uses posture, ears, eyes, breathing, growls, barks, whining, scenting, staring, pawing, positioning, refusal, leaning, carrying/placing objects and deliberate physical choices.

**How to write communication:** state the observable behavior first. Another character may interpret it, but keep the interpretation attributed to that observer, fallible and distinguishable from objective fact. Do not put quotation marks around imagined human thoughts. Do not add subtitles or captions that translate dog thoughts into English.

**Nonverbal social participation:** he can respond to spoken voices via learned words, tone, gaze, body orientation, pointing, proximity, actual sound/scent and routine. Distinct voices may attract, settle, annoy, confuse or be ignored. A stare, sigh, paw, lean, refusal, chosen position, carrying an object or leaving can change a conversation without becoming a compulsory punchline.

**Silent fourth-wall awareness:** bounded permission allows Mana to look toward the audience/camera, bark/growl/react to Black Mimir, use timing that acknowledges presentation or deliberately disengage from frame-level attention. This grants no human speech, telepathy, script access, future knowledge, source-file access or universal actor knowledge. Daynah does not inherit Mana’s silent fourth-wall permission.
